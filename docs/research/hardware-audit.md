# Hardware Capability Audit: Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75C for Orb

**Target Device:** Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75C (1.75" Round AMOLED, CNC Aluminum Enclosure)  
**SoC:** Espressif ESP32-S3R8 (Xtensa 32-bit LX7 dual-core @ 240 MHz, 512 KB SRAM, 8 MB Octal PSRAM, 32 MB Flash)  
**Audit Purpose:** Comprehensive component-by-component hardware inventory, firmware usage audit, concrete unused capabilities for an Apple-quality round desk companion, and top 12 ranked feature recommendations.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Component Bus Architecture

The Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75C is a compact round desk/wearable development device designed around the circular 466×466 AMOLED display. The firmware architecture currently runs a thin-client OS (**Orb**) connecting over pinned-TLS WebSocket to a Mac mini hub running Claude Code, Codex, Dot, and Apple Home bridges.

## 3. Top 12 Ranked Unused Features by Value and Effort
