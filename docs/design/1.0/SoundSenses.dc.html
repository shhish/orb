Sound

Sound stands in for haptics: there's no motor. Sounds are sampled, not synthesised beeps: short, warm, quiet and never musical, except alarms. Silent mutes everything but alarms and timers. Always On is silent. The ES8311 limiter (DRC) is always on, so the small speaker never clips.

Senses

Every input maps to one meaning everywhere. Motion and sound never approve, send or unlock anything.

Hardware we now use

  • The gyroscope (off today) for twist and steadier tilt in games.
  • The second microphone (unread today), and the codec's limiter, volume and EQ.
  • The display's 8-colour idle mode and partial refresh for Always On, if the board measurements confirm the power savings.
  • The battery's current and temperature for health and charge limits.
  • Later: Bluetooth as a Mac media knob when Wi-Fi is down, and the microSD slot.
