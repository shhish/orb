Follow the circleRows fit the chord. Titles curve along the rim instead of truncating. Rings and gauges live on the rim.
One size per roleEvery standalone round control is 80. Focus is a ring, never a bigger disc.
One primaryAt most one tinted primary per screen, centred on the bottom arc. Everything else is neutral glass.
The centre is contentSystem gestures start at the edge. A drag in the middle only scrolls, taps or drags what is under it.
No hidden globalsThe rim drives what is on screen, or nothing. Volume lives in Control Center. Global actions are physical buttons.
It answersEvery touch presses, every step ticks, every place opens from where you touched it and closes back into it.

In a group every disc is the same size, and focus adds a 3 px white ring 4 px outside it.
APP ICONS
Apple's watch icons and SF Symbols are licensed for Apple platforms only, so Orb keeps its own: a flat accent disc with a Framework7 glyph at 46% of the disc, Semibold weight. The glyph is white, or black on the light accents (yellow, teal, mint), by luminance. No gradients or glows.

At most one primary per screen, centred on the bottom arc (centre y 380), with secondary actions beside it on the same arc or as a secondary capsule below. No text-only buttons: "Done" is a secondary capsule. Two glyph buttons sit symmetrically on the bottom arc at ±42° (Timer: cancel, pause). Pressed: scale to 94% in 80 ms and the glass brightens, released with a 160 ms spring.

THE RIM
Rim titles curve along the top on a 200 px baseline, centred at 12 o'clock, within 120° (about 20 characters): a title names the place, and counts belong in the content. Longer titles step down to Footnote 2, then ellipsise. When a full rim ring is drawn (Timer, a light), the title sits inside it on a 176 px baseline. Bottom rim captions read upright on a 212 px baseline. Scroll position is a 4 px arc on the right rim at radius 224, outside the widest rows. Page dots sit inside any rim ring.
GESTURES · the centre is content
A system gesture starts within 40 px of the bezel and moves inward. Travel along that band is the crown. A drag that starts inside it belongs to the screen: it never opens Notifications, the Stack, Control Center or Back. Only the Face, which has nothing to scroll, also swipes between places from the centre. Over 10 px of travel is a scroll, never a tap.
