# Delight implementation and evidence

The merged-board follow-up, current ambient limits, tests and fresh design-gate
captures are in [DELIGHT_BOARD_RESULTS.md](DELIGHT_BOARD_RESULTS.md).

Branch: `orb/w2-delight`. Git was read-only; all work is uncommitted. No board, owner configuration, live hub, launchd, Keychain, or agent session was touched.

## Implementation

- `firmware/components/orb_shell`: app/source/state accents and primary tints; accessibility palette; living Face ring, side complications, minute pixel shifts; local press/launch/reverse/paging/count/check/reward helpers; scroll momentum/elasticity and a rim indicator that holds 600 ms then fades over 150 ms; real voice level ring and transcript Send motion; truthful queued/confirmed receipts and all-clear; registry-backed Play list/empty/launch/error/Close.
- New `firmware/components/orb_sound`: seven quiet synthesized 48–128 ms cues through the ES8311 board API. UI requests never block. One-slot/drop-when-busy queue, Silent and face-down cancellation, voice-priority exclusive audio lease, all acquired resources released between cues. Cue synthesis and failures are sanitizer tested.
- Minimal shared wiring: `firmware/main/main.c` forwards gravity/idle brightness and consumes/publishes app requests on main; `orb_voice.c` and its CMake dependency acquire/release the audio lease. The shell never calls game lifecycle callbacks under LVGL. Registry manifests are immutable after startup.
- `sim`: real registry with storage-unavailable host adapter, static before/after matrices, real-input motion recorder, frame-level clock/Back guards, AX3/opaque/contrast fixtures and UI regressions. `orb_ui`, `orb_display`, LVGL and `orb_apps` internals are unchanged.

### Gate interpretation and limits

- **Layers:** black content; glass only on navigation/actions, at most three glass controls and one tinted primary. Risky approvals retain equal neutral Allow and Deny controls.
- **Canvas:** true black at rest; no gradients or glows. The transient app launch disc is the deliberate exception specified by delight §2.
- **Round-first:** side complications, 6-o'clock Apps, diagonal Back, chord-aware rows and prose; long AX3 titles re-fit between toolbar controls even after staged construction.
- **Sizes:** semantic Inter/Nunito/Framework7, tabular count digits, AX3 and Bold Text reflow, 72 px normal / 46 px minimum targets with visible paging alternatives. The small battery digits retain the HIG's explicit status-widget exception.
- **Colour:** semantic system palette and increased variants for transparency/contrast. Agent lists reserve orange plus one priority hue (error, completion, source), neutralizing other glyphs while keeping their symbols/words. Face prioritizes attention/critical power over working blue when necessary.
- **Approvals/agents:** no default, no unsolicited dismissal of unanswered decisions, no spinner. Working segments are fixed arcs with a slow intensity shimmer and the last step stays static text.
- **Motion/accessibility:** shell springs integrate at 240 Hz; normal page bounce, RM 150 ms fades, no RM zoom/shake/tilt/ambient shimmer; actual mic amplitude still changes its arc value with a fixed RM diameter. Reduce Transparency is opaque; tint labels use black emphasized ink, tested against actual rendered pixels.
- **Gesture alternatives:** Back, Apps, review, next/more, Stop/Cancel/Send, Hold to Talk and game Close remain visible; PWR exit stays main-owned.
- **Apple-made review:** independent checks caught and corrected Face battery overlap, mic-ring centering, duplicate header mic, transient raster rectangles, counter clipping, staged AX3 title overlap and tinted-label contrast.

Two document precedence choices remain explicit: the launcher must show all six prescribed app identity colours even though delight otherwise caps a screen at two accents; and the white-glyph recipe conflicts with the HIG’s minimum symbol contrast on bright discs. Contrast takes precedence: white stays on dark discs; clean black ink replaces it only where white would fall below 3:1. The prescribed flat palette remains intact. A tested black keyline was rejected in independent review as too outlined and removed.

Shared glass press geometry remains HIG §2.2's existing 6%, which wins over delight's 4%; the shared renderer was outside this scope. Risky approvals are never tinted before a choice. Choice feedback starts immediately, but the 600 ms return and 2-second all-clear follow authoritative accepted/delivered results; queued, failed, rejected or competing-controller answers retain their receipts. This preserves HIG agency and the existing delivery contract.

Always On is currently inferred from a lit brightness ≤10 because the existing power model exposes no AOD flag. Its ring updates at 1 Hz. A quiet/RM Face remains stationary except for minute burn-in shifts. Hardware loudness, optical quality, frame rate, heap headroom and reliable audio cancellation latency are unmeasured. A faulty codec write can consume the existing SDK's 1 s timeout; voice acquisition then fails safely at its 160 ms lease deadline. Sibling `orb_games` implementations are not merged: current host evidence launches the real registered Marble controller and tests empty Play separately.
